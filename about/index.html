About

DM

HCLTech · Silicon Valley

Devangshu Mitra

Product Manager, Life Sciences & Life Sciences & Healthcare AI at HCLTech.

I build trust-critical AI products that operate at the convergence layer of healthcare systems, hyperscalers, and frontier models. Currently leading a portfolio of 10+ GenAI products at HCLTech — from drug discovery and patient recruitment to adverse event detection and regulatory intelligence.

Beyond core product execution, I act as an Ecosystem Navigator. I decode the fast-moving signal across EHR giants, LLM providers, and regulatory bodies to build products that survive contact with reality in high-stakes environments.

Before product management, I worked across engagement management and enterprise consulting, giving me a ground-level understanding of how large health systems actually adopt technology. I studied at the University of Texas at Dallas, and I'm based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

I write about enterprise AI, life sciences & healthcare product management, and first-principles thinking — the stuff I wish someone had written when I was figuring it out.
