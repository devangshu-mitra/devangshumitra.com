Long Bio (150 words)

Devangshu Mitra is an AI Product Leader at HCLTech, where he builds and ships 10+ GenAI products across Life Sciences & Healthcare — from drug discovery and clinical trial matching to adverse event detection and regulatory intelligence. He works at the convergence of hyperscalers, frontier model providers, and enterprise health systems, deploying AI in environments where failure has real consequences. Before product management, he worked in enterprise consulting and engagement management, giving him ground-level understanding of how large health systems actually adopt technology. He writes about AI product craft, enterprise GenAI, and first-principles thinking. Based in the San Francisco Bay Area.
